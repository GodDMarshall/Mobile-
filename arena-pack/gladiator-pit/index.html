Weapons rain from the sky. Last one standing takes the glory.

Options
Two buttons each. Your gladiator runs forward on their own. Hold ⟳ to turn, tap ⚔ to strike. Your buttons sit at your edge of the screen, in your color, facing you.
Grab weapons by running over them when your hands are empty. Watch the shadows: whatever falls on your head hurts.
Gladius fast slashes · Spear long reach · Hammer slow and crushing · Net throw it to tangle a rival. Weapons break after a few hits.
After 45 seconds the fire ring closes in. Last one standing wins the round.
Keyboard (turn / strike): Red ZX · Blue NM · Green QW · Gold OP · Violet 12 · Orange 90 · Teal ←↓ · Pink →↑. Esc pauses.
Gamepads: pad 1 is Red, pad 2 is Blue, and so on. Left stick steers, A strikes.
Tap a fighter above to switch between Player, Bot and Off.
