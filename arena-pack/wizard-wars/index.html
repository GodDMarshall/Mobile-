WIZARD
WARS

Fling fireballs, time your shield, and be the last mage standing on a rock that is melting into lava.

How to play

MOVE: hold to walk where your wand points. Let go and your wizard spins, so release when you face the way you want.

CAST: tap to throw a fireball. Hold to raise a shield. Raise it just as a fireball lands to reflect it back at whoever threw it.

Hits knock you further the more hurt you are. Lava burns fast, and the rock crumbles from the edge in.

Keyboard: P1 Q W or arrows + Space, P2 O P, P3 C V, P4 N M. Gamepads: stick moves and aims, A casts.

Wizards

Tap a wizard to switch between player and bot.
