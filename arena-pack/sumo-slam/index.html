Party sumo for 2, 4 or 8 on one screen

Sumo
Slam

Charge across the clay and shove your rivals past the straw rope. The ring shrinks every second, so nobody gets to hide.

Wrestlers
Tap a seat to switch player or CPU
How to play
  • An arrow circles your wrestler. Hold SLAM to freeze it and build power, then let go to charge that way. Every charge flips the arrow's spin.
  • BRACE plants your feet for a moment so you get heavy and hard to move. It needs a few seconds to recover.
  • Cross the straw rope and you're out. Last wrestler inside takes the round. First to 3 rounds wins the match.
  • Push someone out and it counts as your slam. Slams close together, or in a row without going out yourself, set off streak callouts.
  • Keys (slam / brace): Red A S, Blue L K, Gold Z X, Green M N, Purple Q W, Orange P O, Cyan 1 2, Pink 0 9.
  • Gamepads join the player seats in order: A slams, B braces, and the left stick aims directly.
