Rooftop Archers

Everyone starts with three arrows on the roofs of a lantern-lit town. Shoot, stomp heads, or snatch an arrow out of the air and send it back. Last archer standing takes the round.

How to play
  • Jump: tap to jump. Tap again in the air to turn around. Your archer runs on their own and turns at walls.
  • Shoot: hold to draw. The aim swings up and down; let go to fire.
  • Catch: tap Shoot just as an arrow reaches you. It joins your quiver.
  • Stomp: land on a head. Run over stuck arrows to pick them up.
  • Keys: P1 W jump S shoot AD run · P2 ↑ ↓ ←→ · P3 I K JL · P4 numpad 8 5 46
  • Gamepads: A jump, X or right trigger shoot, stick to run and aim.
