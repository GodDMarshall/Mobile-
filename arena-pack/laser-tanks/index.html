LASER TANKS

Bounce lasers through the maze. One hit and you are scrap. Careful: your own shots can get you too.

Turn your device sideways for the best view.

Tap a seat to switch it between Human and Bot.
Touch: every player gets two buttons on their edge of the screen. Hold ↻ to spin in place, tap ✸ to fire. Your tank drives forward by itself.
Keyboard: P1 WASD+Space · P2 arrows+Enter · spin/fire: P3 ZX · P4 CV · P5 BN · P6 M, · P7 12 · P8 90 · Esc pause
Gamepads: stick steers, A fires, B spins. Pad 1 is P1, pad 2 is P2, and so on.
Lasers bounce 8 times. Grab spread for triple shots and shield to survive one hit.
Points: +1 per kill, +1 for surviving the round, −1 for blasting yourself.
