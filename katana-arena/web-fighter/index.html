CRIMSON KATANA

Blood. Steel. Eight blades.

Mode
Fighters
CPU Rounds
Gore Sound

Touch: d-pad moves, up jumps, down crouches. Slash, Kick, Block (hold), Special. Crouch + Slash is the uppercut, crouch + Kick sweeps, toward + Slash up close throws. Slash three times for a combo. After the final round, get close and press Special for the Deathblow.
Keys: left side WASD + F G H T, right side arrows + K L ; O. Gamepads work too. Hold your phone sideways.
