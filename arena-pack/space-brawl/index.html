How to brawl

  • Your ship always flies forward. Hold TURN to spin, press FIRE to shoot. You hold 3 shots and they recharge.
  • Take a hit and your pilot ejects. As a pilot, hold TURN to spin in place, let go to run, and press FIRE for a tiny blaster.
  • Survive 7 seconds on foot and you rebuild your ship. Shoot a ship as a pilot to steal the kill and hijack a fresh ship.
  • Ships flatten pilots. The last pilot alive wins the round, and the first to 3 rounds is champion.
  • Blast rocks for power-ups: Triple shot and Shield. After 50 seconds it's sudden death: no rebuilds and faster reloads.
  • Tap a BOT pad mid-match to take over that seat.
Gamepads: pad 1 drives seat 1 and so on. A / RT fires, B / LB / RB or the stick turns.
